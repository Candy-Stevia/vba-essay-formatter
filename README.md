# AcademicFormatter v1.0.8 — Admonition Update

A local, deterministic Microsoft Word Desktop (Windows) Add-in built for Vietnamese and English essays, reports, and theses. It standardizes document layout and lists, converts supported LaTeX formulas into Word equations, audits style consistency across merged team drafts, and applies citation/style patches generated by your AI Web Chat—all offline inside Word without uploading your files to any external server.

Everything runs strictly on explicit rules, patterns, and bookmarks. When something looks ambiguous, it skips or highlights it so you stay in control.

## What It Actually Does

- **Formatting, Lists & PDF Export:** `Format All` applies your chosen font, size, spacing, and indent to body text while formatting Headings (L1–L4), Table/Figure captions, and `Source:`/`Nguồn:` lines and rebuilding TOC, LOT, and LOF with synced page numbers (including Roman numerals). `Update Lists` refreshes headings and page numbers without touching body paragraphs, and `Export PDF` creates a PDF with clean Heading Bookmarks.
- **Teamwork-Friendly Linking:** `Link Cross-Refs Only` adds clickable cross-references (`Bảng`, `Hình`, `Sơ đồ`, `Chương`, `Mục`, `Phụ lục`...) without changing a single font, size, Outline level, or native Word TOC field. `AI Patch` links APA citations across both main body and Appendices (including identical table cells) without requiring boundary markers.
- **Pre-Submission Style Linter & Style Capture:** `Pre-Audit X-Ray & Lint` runs a read-only audit showing document structure, every paragraph deviating from the majority style across 8 scopes, unmatched visual headings, and EN/VI APA citation counts—with 1-click full Clipboard export for your AI. `Capture Style` learns and saves your document's existing stepped typography (skipping `TIE` conflicts) as a sparse `@STYLE` profile.
- **LaTeX-to-Word Math:** `Build Math` converts supported LaTeX (`$...$`, `$$...$$`, `\(...\)`, `\[...\]`, matrices, `align`, `cases`, borderless `array`, etc.) into native Word `OMath` with Pastel review shading. Use `Toggle / Edit` to switch any equation back to raw LaTeX, `Next Pink` to jump to flagged items, and `Finalize Math` to clear shading before submission.
- **Safety Nets:** Single-step **`Ctrl + Z`** Undo on every major action, **`ESC`** to cancel long runs safely with rollback, automatic Track Changes pause/restore, and an ephemeral `_crash_backup_` copy that deletes itself on clean completion.

## How to Install & Uninstall (Windows Only)

1. Go to the **[Releases](../../releases)** page and download **`AcademicFormatter_v1.0.8.zip`**.
2. Extract the zip folder anywhere on your computer.
3. Close all open Microsoft Word windows.
4. Double-click **`install-or-update.bat`** (checks GitHub for a newer `.dotm` and `instruction-108.md` when online, falls back to local files if offline/blocked, unblocks Windows flags, and copies `AcademicFormatter.dotm` into your Word `STARTUP` folder).
5. Open Word. You will see the **`Formatter Macro`** tab on the Ribbon.

To remove the add-in at any time, close all Word windows and double-click **`Uninstall.bat`**.

## Using AI to Prep or Audit Your Draft (Optional)

This feature is tricky but **you don't need to read `instruction-108.md` yourself.** AI web chat can read it and guide you step by step instead:

1. Attach **both `instruction-108.md` (included in the zip) AND your `.docx` manuscript/thesis** (or paste your draft) into the chat — the AI needs your real text to check headings/citations and generate exact patches.
2. Tell the AI in plain language what you want (e.g., *"format this from scratch"*, *"only link APA citations & cross-refs without touching my team's fonts/TOC"*, or *"audit inconsistent styles before submission"*).
3. Follow the AI's click-by-click instructions in Word (for existing or group drafts, it may ask you to click **`Formatter Macro -> AI Patch -> Pre-Audit X-Ray & Lint`** and paste the Clipboard report back so it can see your actual font sizes and TOC). When pasting plain drafts from chat into Word, always use **`Ctrl + Shift + V`** (*Keep Text Only*) so `$` signs are not escaped into `\$`.

## Tested Scope & Honest Limitations

- **Tested Scope:** Verified with 20 automated checks on Microsoft Word Desktop (Windows) covering static VBA linting, Word COM formatting/idempotency/Undo integration, golden OMML LaTeX math cases, teamwork/style-linter fixtures, a 500-paragraph stress test, and the author's real essays.
- **Limitations:** Works **only on Microsoft Word Desktop for Windows (Word 2013 or newer)**—not macOS, Word Online, or mobile. Documents with heavily merged/nested tables, exotic custom Word fields, or non-standard layouts may still hit edge cases; by design, the add-in skips ambiguous items safely rather than guessing. Usually this macro runs in under 10 seconds on most documents; however, as tested on a real 120-page document, total run time hit 35 seconds on the author's student laptop (including formatting all levels + building TOC, LOF, and LOT).
